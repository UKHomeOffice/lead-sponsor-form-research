Tell us about a community group that wants to sponsor a refugee

Use this service to tell us about a communituy group that wants to sponsor a refugee.

Who can use this service

You can only use this service if you are an accredited lead sponsor.

Before you start

You will need:

  • your lead sponsor accreditation number
  • the name of the community group you want to tell us about
  • information about 5 group members who will be most involved in supporting the refugee or family (name, date of birth and nationality, how you checked this information was correct
  • mobile phone numbers for 2 of the 5 group members
  • information about the community group's suitability to support the refugee or family
  • information about the funding arrangements
  • information about the proposed accommodation arrangements
  • details of any risks or concerns and how they will be managed
  • names of the refugee or family

You will not be able to save this form.
