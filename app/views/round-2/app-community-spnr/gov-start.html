Tell us about an organisation that wants to sponsor a refugee

Use this service to tell us about a community group or university that wants to sponsor a refugee.

Who can use this service

You can only use this service if you are an accredited lead sponsor.

Before you start

You will need different information depending on whether you want to tell us about a community group or university.

Community groups

You will need:

  • your lead sponsor accreditation number
  • the name of the community group you want to tell us about (if the group does not have a name, they will need to choose one before you start the application)
  • information about 5 community group members who will be most involved in supporting the refugee or family (name, date of birth and nationality)
  • mobile phone numbers for 2 of the 5 group members, in case of an emergency
  • information about the community group's suitability to support the refugee or family
  • information about the money available to support the refugee or family, or any plans to fundraise
  • information about the proposed accommodation arrangements
  • details of any risks or concerns and how you will manage them
  • names and date of birth of the refugee or family
  • the form reference number from a previous sponsorship application, if the community group has sponsored a refugee before

Universities

You will need:

  • your lead sponsor accreditation number
  • the name of the university
  • their student sponsor licence details
  • the name and contact details for the person responsible for the sponsorship arrangement within the university
  • information about the proposed course or university placement
  • information about proposed accommodation arrangements
  • information about pastoral, welfare and academic support
  • information about tuition fees, living costs and other funding arrangements
  • information about any funding shortfalls and how you will cover them
  • details of any risks or concerns you identified and how you will manage them
  • the form reference number from a previous sponsorship application, if the university has sponsored a refugee before

You will not be able to save this form.
