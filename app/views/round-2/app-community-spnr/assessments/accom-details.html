Accommodation suitability

Tell us how you assessed whether the accommodation is suitable for the refugee or family.

You can include:

  • any checks you carried out on the accommodation
  • any evidence you reviewed to confirm the accommodation is suitable
  • the condition of the accommodation
  • the size of the accommodation and whether it provides enough living space
  • how you checked the accommodation meets any relevant housing or safety requirements
  • any accessibility features or adaptations
  • access to local services, public transport and community support
